# Wave 1, test stage (2026-10-27): 1137 runs of 112 species

`wave_driver.py` (this folder's parent) planned 112 species (panel-ready, MAT1-1/MAT1-2 naming, `wave1_species.tsv`), built a lineage panel set for each from the BFD assemblies, chose up to 20 runs per species (natural-isolate studies, 0.6 to 12 Gb, 4 per study at most) and typed them on the HPCC (`exfab` and `stajichlab` partitions, 1 CPU and 6 GB per task, about 76 tasks at once; the `exfab` account allows 32 CPUs). Files: `gates.tsv` (decision and numbers per species), `results_all.tsv` (one row per run), `summary_by_group.tsv`, `SUMMARY.md`.

## Result
- Runs: 1127 finished of 1137; 9 unavailable at NCBI (ENA-only), 1 failed download. Calls: MAT1-1 499, MAT1-2 441, both 30, none 105, low_depth 52.
- Against `detect` (the idiomorph of a BFD assembly with the same BioSample): 481 runs have a truth, 437 were typed, **434 agree and 3 disagree** (44 untyped). The three disagreements: *Macrophomina phaseolina* 14-48 (MAT1-1 call, truth MAT1-2, MAT1-2 core depth 0), *Aureobasidium melanogenum* FJII-L3-CM-P1 (MAT1-1 call, truth MAT1-2) and *Ustilaginoidea virens* UV-GVT (MAT1-1 depth 2.9 against MAT1-2 0.6, truth MAT1-2). *Aureobasidium* is homothallic with both MAT genes in one locus, where `detect`'s own idiomorph label is a known weak point (issue #138), so these may be the truth's error, not the typing's.
- Gate decisions: {'PASS': 36, 'PROVISIONAL': 38, 'HOLD': 38} (PASS needs at least 5 typed runs with a truth and 95% agreement; PROVISIONAL has fewer truth runs; PROVISIONAL_HOMOTHALLIC has at least half `both`; thresholds in `wave_driver.py`). 38 species are held, mostly for a low typed fraction (many `none` or `low_depth`: a panel from few assemblies does not match divergent strains) and a few because most runs read `both`, which is the expected answer for a homothallic species and now gets its own status. Five more were held before any download (too few runs or a core too small).
## Full stage (77 species that passed the test gate, up to 50 runs each; `results_all_full.tsv`, `gates_full.tsv`, `WAVE_REPORT.md`)
- 1,292 runs: 1,277 done, 15 unavailable at NCBI (ENA-only), 0 failed; 2,003 GB transferred from 418 studies. Calls: MAT1-1 590, MAT1-2 601, both 42, none 22, low_depth 22 (93% typed).
- Against `detect`: 523 runs with a truth, 505 typed, **503 agree, 2 disagree**. Both disagreements are *Dothistroma septosporum* (SRR5420328 and SRR5420329, consecutive accessions, each called the opposite of its truth: a swapped sample label is more likely than two typing errors, not checked).
- Gate on the full data: PASS 44, PROVISIONAL 28, PROVISIONAL_HOMOTHALLIC 3, HOLD 7 (4 held before download, *Aureobasidium pullulans* core too small, *D. septosporum* 7 of 9 agree, *Fusarium lateritium* typed 0.67); the 30 species held at the test gate were not run further.
- Best-sampled species (MAT1-1 / MAT1-2 calls): *Aspergillus niger* 36/14, *Exophiala dermatitidis* 10/40, *A. fumigatus* 24/25, *Fusarium oxysporum* 20/27, *Beauveria bassiana* 25/22, *Neurospora discreta* 30/16, *Coccidioides posadasii* 18/28. These are counts of sampled strains, not population frequencies.

## Not checked
Whether the many `none` and `low_depth` runs in the held species are divergent lineages (a second lineage panel would help), mislabelled species or non-WGS libraries; the held species are the work list. Strain counts are not population frequencies: related or clonal strains are not removed (4 runs per study is the only guard).

## Rescue passes and the final table (`results_all_final.tsv`, `gates_final.tsv`, `merge_rescues.py`)
Runs untyped at k=31 against their species panel (`none`: the core had diverged; `low_depth`: the flank did not match) were typed again:
- **k=21 fallback** (`FALLBACK_K=21` in the worker). On 278 runs of 47 species it typed 41 of 168 runs that were untyped at k=31 (35 of 91 `none` runs with the idiomorph present, 6 of 57 `low_depth`, 0 of 20 with the idiomorph absent; 12 of the 13 with a truth agree) and lost 6 of 106 controls that had typed at k=31 (the 100 that stayed typed all agree). So k=21 is a fallback only. Files: `../kexp/` is not committed (the k=21 and k=25 result rows are in the HPCC `waves/wave1_kexp/out`).
- **Genus panels** (`panel_from_assemblies.py --species "Genus " --diverse`, up to 12 lineages spanning the most species, 25 genera): of 139 runs still untyped, 28 were typed (`results_genus_panels.tsv`), 8 of 8 with a truth agree; of 45 controls, 39 typed and 38 of the 39 agree (the one disagreement, *Ustilaginoidea virens* SRR8399124, was already discordant).
- **Final, all passes merged:** 1,629 runs done of 1,646; 90.3% typed (MAT1-1 743, MAT1-2 728, both 49, none 64, low_depth 45; 1,459 typed by the species panel, 43 by the k=21 fallback, 18 by a genus panel). 640 runs have a `detect` truth: 605 typed, **599 agree, 6 disagree** (99.0%): *Dothistroma septosporum* x2, *Macrophomina phaseolina*, *Aureobasidium melanogenum*, *Ustilaginoidea virens* and one k=21 rescue, *Fusarium proliferatum* SRR31408079 (MAT1-1 call at 10x with MAT1-2 at 0, truth MAT1-2; not explained).
- **Gate (final data):** PASS 44, PROVISIONAL 35, PROVISIONAL_HOMOTHALLIC 3, HOLD 30 (this line said 36 and 29 until 2026-10-28; `gates_final.tsv` has 35 and 30; 5 of them held before any download). The remaining holds are mostly species with 20 to 45% of runs still untyped (*Fusarium proliferatum*, *Fusarium solani*, *Aspergillus parasiticus*, *Trichoderma harzianum*) and a few with disagreements.
- Worker bugs found and fixed on the way: a shell variable (`c1`) shared between the fallback and the convert-seconds field wrote a call name into `run.json` (records repaired by hand, the typing results were not affected); a time-out left a truncated `run.json` (the summarizer now treats it as `failed_unfinished`).
