## Traps a fresh session will hit

* **Code and database must come from the same frozen worktree on /bigdata.**
  Both runners now enforce this; a hand-written job must set `PYTHONPATH` AND
  `MATPREDICT_DB_ROOT`. Verify from a report (`_reference.faa` lists the records
  searched; `routing_error`, `genetic_code` fields exist only on new code).
* **Never edit a worktree a job is running from.** Make a new `run-<sha>`
  worktree per launch; several exist under `.claude/worktrees/run-*`.
* **CPU group limit**: other projects share the account's allocation; small
  jobs may pend on `AssocGrpCpuLimit`. `scontrol update` cannot move a job to
  `short` (QOS); cancel and resubmit.
* **Record annotation errors** in `ANNOTATION_ERRORS_FIXED_REPORT.md` as they
  are found (curator's standing request).
