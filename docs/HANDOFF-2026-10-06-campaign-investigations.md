## Findings you can rely on (details in the notes)
1. **Polish cap (merged):** admitted clusters with best identity >= 50% are always polished (`--polish-strong-identity`, default 50; 0 = plain cap 6). Replay over 2,722 genomes: 0 lost loci versus 17 for the plain cap at the same polishing work.
2. **PR identification has not been improved in the pipeline.** Sequence ML (k-mers, Smith-Waterman, ESM-2 8M to 650M) scores at or below the simple rule "strict CAAX within 10 kb" (AUC 0.80) on held-out orders; the "no" is underpowered (20 mating / 64 other loci, 4 orders, 12 species), not proven. "CAAX within 10 kb OR flank gene within 50 kb" reaches AUC 0.98 without learning, but the flank signal is semi-circular and untested outside the lineages that supplied the queries.
3. **Tremellomycetes failures** (Tremellales, Trichosporonales, Filobasidiales, Cystofilobasidiales): every good-assembly failure has a STE3-like locus and a withheld cluster; the gate is `modelled_gene_bar` under phylum-fallback routing (about 300 noise clusters per genome in the three sister orders). Called and uncalled genomes show the same HD-STE3 distances. Rearrangement was not tested beyond contig and distance (no orientation or FAO1/PAN6 synteny).
4. **Receptor arrays in Agaricomycetes** (1,287 quality-passing genomes, 705 species): median 9 STE3-like loci per genome; 80% of 9,436 arrays are singletons; 57% of loci in 50 kb arrays are expected from contig fragmentation alone, so only arrays of 3 or more loci are reliable. 1,039 PR calls (944 unverified); 804 of the 944 have array support, 140 do not (73 in Cantharellales). 627 CAAX-flagged arrays have no call, and 37% (32 to 43%) of those are chance-level by an array-level null. Arrays hold both mating and non-mating receptors (*C. cinerea*, *S. commune*), so array membership does not establish a mating receptor.
5. **B loci:** in 6 of 6 curated genomes all B receptors sit in one array, but array span and size do not identify a B locus (the *C. cinerea* array is 77 kb for a 21.5 kb locus; tandem receptors are merged into one locus). A "cassette" (receptor with >= 2 strict-CAAX ORFs within 5 kb) is in 4 of 6 B arrays and 0 of 25 other arrays, but only 2 genomes have independent labels. 273 cassette loci have no PR call (about 13 expected by chance).
6. **Reference gaps:** about 1,460 excess misses (6.4% of 22,685 Ascomycota and Basidiomycota genomes) in 15 orders; Microascales/*Ceratocystis* is the one clear quick win from deposited sequences; Botryosphaeriales is not reference-limited. See `analysis/2026-10-05_reference-gap-triage.md`.
7. **Dothideomycetes:** SLA2 is detached from the MAT locus in most lineages (14% inside the called locus versus 80% in Sordariomycetes): mostly real biology, not missed detection.
