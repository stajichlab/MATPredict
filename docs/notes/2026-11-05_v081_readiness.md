## The S. pombe regression (#254, #252)*
In the two genomes with a lone P cassette (GCA_006386205.1, GCA_036558985.1), v0.8.0 calls it `mat1` P and #252 relabels it `mat2` P. The lone P on its own contig, with no M cassette within 11 kb, is most likely the expressed `mat1`; the silent mat2-P / mat3-M pair is the 13 kb region on another contig, which is called only `mat1` M (six more genomes). The records are cassette-only, so identity cannot tell mat1 from mat2: only arrangement can. The CHANGELOG line "the silent P donor cassette is `mat2` again in the two genomes that carry it" is probably backwards. Options: restore the v0.8.0 labels and mark S. pombe cassette labels unverified (recommended, with the arrangement rule and the missing silent P in the 13 kb region as v0.9.0 work with #254); build the arrangement rule now; or ship as is with a corrected CHANGELOG.
