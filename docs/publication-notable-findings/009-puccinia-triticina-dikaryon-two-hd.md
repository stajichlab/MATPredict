# 009. Rust dikaryons show two HD loci

- **Category:** hybrid; biology
- **Status:** candidate
- **Lineage:** Basidiomycota, Pucciniomycotina, Pucciniales

## Summary
With the first curated rust HD (bE/bW) references, Puccinia triticina 19NSW04
is called with two HD loci, as a dikaryon carrying two nuclei should be.

## Evidence
- `docs/notes/2026-09-26_pucciniomycotina-curation.md` (curation-puccinio):
  P. triticina GCF_026914185.1 and 19NSW04 called at 78-83%, "the latter with
  two HD loci"; four Phakopsora pachyrhizi genomes with two loci each (bE 49%,
  bW 31-33%; distant hits, gene order not checked).
- Records `rustHD` (tier 2, Cuomo et al. 2017, PMID 27913634).
- 19NSW04 accession: unverified in the note (strain name only).

## Method that found it
Detection with the rustHD family (curation-puccinio `293640d`).

## Verification done / still open
Open: confirm the two loci are on different haplotigs and carry different
bE/bW alleles.

## Limits
Distant Phakopsora calls not checked for synteny.
