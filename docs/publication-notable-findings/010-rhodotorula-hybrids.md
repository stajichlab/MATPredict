# 010. Rhodotorula hybrids: one called A1+A2 with two HD loci, one missed

- **Category:** hybrid
- **Status:** verified for CCT 0783 (matches the study); RIT389 is a known miss
- **Lineage:** Basidiomycota, Sporidiobolales

## Summary
Among the strains with published-preprint assignments, two are A1/A2 hybrids.
R. toruloides CCT 0783 is called A1+A2 with two HD calls, as reported. R.
mucilaginosa RIT389 gets no P/R call (one HD call).

## Evidence
- `docs/notes/2026-09-27_rhodotorula-curation.md` (curation-puccinio),
  "Known answers" section.
- CCT 0783 (R. toruloides): GCA_016808315.1 -- called A1+A2 with two HD loci.
- RIT389 (R. mucilaginosa): GCA_002250355.1 -- no P/R call.
  (Accessions from BFD samples.csv, verified 2026-09-27. The hybrid status of
  both strains comes from the unpublished manuscript; cite the preprint
