MTLA1 polish status in the v0.9.0 Ascomycota campaign (#260), 2026-11-21.
Jobs: 32359555 (MTLA1 gene entry of every Ascomycota:MTL locus in results/2026-11-09_v090_ascomycota/reports_all.tar.zst, 1,661 entries, 1,533 genomes, 40 min) and 32360062 (probe, 30 s; earlier runs 32360040 and 32360056 used a wrong miniprot option and an incomplete reference set and are discarded).
Probe: one genome per species for 24 species with an unpolished live MTLA1 (genera Lodderomyces, Sungouiella, Yamadazyma, Scheffersomyces, Metschnikowia; first 24 by genome id) plus 4 polished_agree controls. (a) Pfam PF00046/PF05920 (homeodomain, gathering cutoff, pyhmmer, six-frame ORFs of the pipeline's MTLA1 hit +-600 bp); (b) miniprot 0.2 -G 5k --gff of the 6 MTLA1 proteins of every MTL_A record in db/ (main f901c929) against the whole genome, best alignment by identity, then the same Pfam test on it.
Tables: mtla1_status_by_genus.tsv (status counts by order and genus), mtla1_unpolished_live.tsv (the 220 entries), probe.tsv (28 genomes). The scripts (mtla1_260.py, mtla1_probe_260.py) are one-offs on the HPCC, results/2026-11-21_ctg_mtla1_260/, not kept in the repository. Genomes are the public BFD assemblies.
