Intron and splice-site census of every MAT1-1-1 and MAT1-2-1 gene model in the v0.9.0 definitive Ascomycota campaign (19,343 reports of results/2026-11-09_v090_ascomycota/reports_all.tar.zst, commit 7d73be5, db run-7d73be5; reports with a MAT1-1-1 or MAT1-2-1 gene that is not `lost_to`), job 32354212 (HPCC, 2 h 30 min), test run on 400 reports 32354168. For each gene with an exon model (14,018 of 15,536): the model's exons on the genome, introns with donor and acceptor on the gene strand, classes GT-AG, GC-AG, AT-AC and other, translation (genetic code of the report), and the genome's own funannotate annotation (Fungi_BFD_runs/genome_annotation, found as in scripts/cap_annotation_review.py): the verdict annotated (an annotated mRNA covers at least half of the model span), partial, novel or no_annotation, whether the best-overlapping annotated mRNA has exactly the model's intron set, and per intron whether that exact intron is in it. Summaries: census262.summary.txt, census262.by_order.tsv, census262.by_genus.tsv; census262.genes_by_class.tsv has one row per modelled gene. The per-intron table (15,480 introns) and the scripts (intron_census_262.py, sum262.py) are one-offs on the HPCC, results/2026-11-21_mat_intron_census_262/, and are not kept in the repository. Basidiomycota and Mucoromycotina have no MAT1-1-1 or MAT1-2-1 genes and were not run. The comparison with the annotation measures agreement, not correctness.
