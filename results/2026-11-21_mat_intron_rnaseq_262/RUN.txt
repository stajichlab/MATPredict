Transcript check of MAT1-1-1 / MAT1-2-1 intron disagreements (#262), 2026-11-21, job 32358493 (HPCC, 6 min).
Sample (sample.tsv, 287 genes): A = MAT1-1-1 model with no intron, annotation has one (212 genes, up to 10 species per order); B = Botryosphaeriales MAT1-2-1, intron set differs (35 genes, up to 2 genomes per species, 22 species); K = controls, model and annotation have the same introns (40 genes, one per species). Seed 262.
Method: window of each gene +-2 kb; minimap2 2.30 -cx splice:hq -G 5k -N 5 --cs of the species' Trinity genome-guided assembly (Fungi_BFD_runs/rnaseq_data/<species>.trinity-GG.fasta) onto the windows; junctions from the cs tag; transcripts overlapping the gene span counted per junction. Verdict: no_transcript_evidence (<2 overlapping transcripts); model_supported (every model intron has >=2 transcripts, or for an intron-less model >=2 unspliced transcripts cover the span, and no other junction has >=2); annotation_supported; other_junctions; inconclusive.
Transcripts are species-level assemblies, not the sequenced strain's reads. The script (rna262.py) is a one-off on the HPCC, results/2026-11-21_mat_intron_rnaseq_262/, not kept in the repository.
