Raw reads

Which mating-type idiomorph does a strain carry, read from its public sequencing reads without assembling a genome. One report per species.

Read this first. Each run is one public read set, typed with matpredict reads-type against a panel built from assemblies of the same species (k-mers found in only one idiomorph, with the shared flank as a depth control). The counts are of the public runs sampled, up to 50 per species and at most 4 per study: they are not population frequencies, and related or repeated strains are not removed from the run counts (species with enough sketched runs show clone groups and counts per clone on their report). Agreement is measured against the detect call on an assembly of the same BioSample, where one exists.

Confidence of each species panel

A species panel is confirmed when at least 75% of its runs get a single-allele call, flank depth is adequate, both idiomorphs together are under 20% of runs, and its calls agree with assembly calls (at least 95%, with five or more comparisons). It is provisional when too few assembly comparisons exist, or the species is homothallic (both idiomorphs expected). It is withheld when any check fails; its reads are listed but no frequencies should be drawn from them.

Curated collections

Method and limits

  • Data: public runs from the NCBI SRA (survey of fungal whole-genome Illumina sets; mutant, cross, RNA-seq and similar sets are excluded; Saccharomyces cerevisiae is skipped). Up to 2 million read pairs per run, streamed and discarded; only the call and depths are kept.
  • Retyping: a run with no call at k = 31 is retried at k = 21, then against a genus panel; the report marks these runs.
  • Clone groups: runs whose k=21 read sketches have an average similarity of at least 0.998 are grouped (species with 10 or more sketched runs). Counts per clone are given only where the number of clones is stable between 0.998 and 0.997. Each report offers the allele counts for all studies pooled or study by study, per run or per clone.
  • Waves: wave 1 covered species with MAT1-1/MAT1-2 panels; wave 2 added the remaining reference-tier A and B species, among them the yeasts (a / alpha and similar names, borrowing loci from congeners where a species had one idiomorph) and Botrytis cinerea typed across all its public strains. In diploid yeasts both idiomorphs are expected in a strain, so a "both" call is not a failure there.
