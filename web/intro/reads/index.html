Raw reads

Which mating-type idiomorph does a strain carry, read from its public sequencing reads without assembling a genome. One report per species.

Read this first. Each run is one public read set, typed with matpredict reads-type against a panel built from assemblies of the same species (k-mers found in only one idiomorph, with the shared flank as a depth control). The counts are of the public runs sampled, up to 700 per species (one run per BioSample; species with fewer public strains have fewer): they are not population frequencies, and related or repeated strains are not removed from the run counts (species with enough sketched runs show clone groups and counts per clone on their report). Agreement is measured against the detect call on an assembly of the same BioSample, where one exists.

Population reports

For some species every public isolate, and the lab's own collection, is kept as a read sketch and typed; these reports give the mating-type frequency per clone (near-identical isolates counted once), by country, study and lineage.

Confidence of each species panel

A species panel is confirmed when at least 75% of its runs get a single-allele call, flank depth is adequate, both idiomorphs together are under 20% of runs, and its calls agree with assembly calls (at least 95%, with five or more comparisons). It is provisional when too few assembly comparisons exist, or the species is homothallic (both idiomorphs expected). It is unvalidated when the panel works for at least half of the runs but misses one of the limits above (fewer than 75% single-allele calls, more than 20% of runs with both idiomorphs, low flank depth, or too few assembly comparisons to judge agreement); its calls are listed with the reason, and allele counts from them need checking before they are used. It is not typable by presence when the genome itself holds both idiomorphs: a homothallic species with both MAT genes in every genome, or a yeast with silent mating-type cassettes (an expressed locus plus silent copies of both idiomorphs). Every strain then reads as carrying both, so its calls are listed but not given as allele frequencies. It is withheld when the calls cannot be used: no panel could be built, too few runs exist, fewer than half the runs get a single-allele call, or the calls disagree with ten or more assembly calls.

Curated collections

Three collections were typed in full, before the public-run survey, with panels built and checked for the species. Their reports follow the same layout. They overlap with the species below (A. fumigatus, C. immitis, C. posadasii) but use more strains, and they are not part of the totals above.

Species from public runs

The alleles are the idiomorph names the species' panel uses (MAT1-1 / MAT1-2 for most filamentous fungi; a / alpha for yeasts; P / M or A / B for others); each is shown with the number of runs typed as that single allele. Reference tier: how the species panel was built (A and A1: curated MAT records; B: records of a close relative; C: panel from the species' own assemblies only). Click a species for its report; every report has a TSV of the runs.

Method and limits

  • Data: public runs from the NCBI SRA (survey of fungal whole-genome Illumina sets; mutant, cross, RNA-seq and similar sets are excluded; Saccharomyces cerevisiae is skipped). Up to 2 million read pairs per run, streamed and discarded; only the call and depths are kept.
  • Retyping: a run with no call at k = 31 is retried at k = 21, then against a genus panel; the report marks these runs.
  • Clone groups: runs whose k=21 read sketches have an average solid-containment similarity of at least 0.9995 are grouped (k-mers seen too few times in a run, mostly sequencing errors, are left out). Counts per clone are given only for species with 10 or more sketched runs where the number of clones is stable between 0.9995 and 0.999, the read subsamples are deep enough (median k-mer depth 8 or more) and the strains are not so alike that the sketch cannot separate them. Each report offers the allele counts for all studies pooled or study by study, per run or per clone.
  • Panels: species with MAT1-1/MAT1-2 panels, and the remaining reference-tier A and B species, among them the yeasts (a / alpha and similar names, borrowing loci from congeners where a species had one idiomorph) and Botrytis cinerea typed across all its public strains. In diploid yeasts both idiomorphs are expected in a strain, so a "both" call is not a failure there.
