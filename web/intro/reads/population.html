Gene and Starship screens

The same sketches answer presence questions for any sequence. These are the standing screens of this store: each target is called from its own specific k-mers (absent from the other targets and from the core shared by 95% of isolates). The truth columns compare with assemblies or a curated set where one exists. A short target (a single gene, a few specific k-mers) gives a coarse call: partial means only some of its k-mers were seen.
