Calls by study

Runs of one study are often one population or one cross; the counts are of runs sampled (at most 4 per study), not of independent strains.

Clone groups

Groups come from a k=21 sketch of each run's reads (up to 2 million read pairs): runs whose average similarity is at least 0.998 are grouped (average linkage). Two samples of one genome often score 0.995 to 0.999, so the threshold cannot tell a copy of a strain from a very close relative with certainty. Method and calibration.
