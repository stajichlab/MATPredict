Download per-genome tables

One row per genome: lineage, call, alleles, locus types, number of loci, best confidence, and each locus as contig:start-end|allele|confidence|locus class. Tab-separated.

    Rows with no value at a rank (for example Mucoromycotina family, which the input tables do not carry) are grouped under “(none)”. Genus and species come from the genome's recorded species name; unrecognised names are “(unassigned)”. “Two alleles” means two distinct idiomorphs were called in one genome: homothallism, silent mating cassettes, a hybrid or mixed assembly, or a paralog. It is a flag, not a ruling. Allele names are as reported per lineage and are not merged. A call is detection, not proof; genome counts reflect uneven strain sampling.
